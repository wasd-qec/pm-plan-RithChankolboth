# Stakeholder Analysis: Realtime Quiz Platform

## Priority Stakeholders (High Influence, High Interest)

The two primary stakeholders possessing both high influence and high interest are **S01 (Head of the IT department - Project Sponsor)** and **S02 (Supervising Lecturer - Project Manager)**. They demand the PM's immediate attention and regular communication because the Sponsor controls budget authorisation, project charter approval, and formal phase-gate acceptance (at week 7 and week 14), while the PM actively directs daily execution, team workflow, and scope adherence. Failing to satisfy either stakeholder immediately compromises the project's viability, resources, or academic progression.

## Veto Stakeholder

**S08 (Data Protection Officer / Registrar)** represents the critical "veto" stakeholder because they possess the unilateral authority to prohibit student participation and cancel the week 14 pilot if student records, grades, or personal identifying data fail to comply with institutional privacy and on-campus data storage standards.
